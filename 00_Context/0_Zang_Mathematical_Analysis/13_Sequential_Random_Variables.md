## **13.5 Historical and Bibliographical Remarks**

Azuma’s inequality (Azuma, 1967) maybe regarded as a generalization of Hoeffding’s inequality with a similar proof. It can be used to prove McDiarmid’s inequality in Chapter 6. Freedman’s inequality (Freedman, 1975) can be regarded as a generalization of Bernstein’s inequality for independent random variables.
